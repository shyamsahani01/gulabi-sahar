# Cinestar and Funstar / Times Square Mall — exterior survey

15 November 2026: **74 actual Google Street View page photographs**, 37 for each site. Each site has a 12-direction main panorama, eight overlapping headings at each of two distinct approach positions, and nine closer entrance/forecourt/upper-facade shots. All photographs were inspected in contact sheets; key facades were inspected at full resolution. Imagery shown is March 2026. These are horizontal 360° directional surveys from six positions, not theater interiors or an end-to-end survey of every adjoining lane.

[Open all local references](../reference-gallery.html). Exact URLs, positions, panorama IDs and hashes: [captures.json](captures.json). Primary Google place links and listing context: [places.json](places.json). Large source images remain local; only manifests, scripts and documentation are pushed to GitHub. Photos are excluded from Android export.

Best roof/front image: `cinestar/upper-035.png`. Approaches show adjoining shops, a partly built concrete-frame building and the connected street.
- **Funstar listing / Times Square Mall area:** long rose facade with tall repeated pilasters and blue/grey glazing, ARG rooftop signage, upper shop gallery/railings, ground-level shops and forecourt parking, trees and approach roads. Best corner image: `funstar/upper-240.png`; broad frontal view: `funstar/approach-west-218.png`. The saved facade shows ARG/Times Square and other tenants; a Funstar exterior sign or interior has not been verified. Do not invent unseen cinema interiors.
